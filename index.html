Who we are

A Schools community connected to a wider movement.

NHSF (UK) Schools is a newer secondary-school initiative launched in 2024 as part of NHSF (UK), the wider National Hindu Students’ Forum (UK), founded on university campuses in 1991. It helps students aged 13 to 18 connect with heritage, explore Hindu Dharma, ask questions and build community through culture, sport, leadership and sewā.

Founded in 1991 by Hindu university students in London. Schools launched in 2024 for secondary students aged 13 to 18.

Every member of the NHSF (UK) Schools team for 2026/27 holds a valid DBS check.

Purpose

Rooted in Dharma, open to learning and community.

NHSF (UK) Schools exists so young British Hindus can grow in identity before university, with a national parivaar behind every local society.

A closer look

Hindu Horizons

Hindu Horizons is an educational and interactive discussion series hosted by the NHSF (UK) School Team. Each session is led by a member of the Sanskaar team.

Purpose

Explore Hindu scriptures, history, festivals and Dharma (moral duty) through real-life dilemmas and everyday choices.

Format

Hosted online through interactive virtual sessions such as Zoom.

Target audience

Mainly young people and teenagers, typically ages 11–18 or secondary school students.

Inclusion and safety come first.

Keep activities welcoming for Hindu and non-Hindu students. Explain traditions simply, respect different Hindu backgrounds, and plan for accessibility and dietary needs. Give students a private way to raise concerns. If there are worries about prejudice, Hinduphobia or wellbeing, speak with a trusted teacher and NHSF.
