NHSF (UK) Schools

Privacy Policy

NHSF (UK) Schools information guide and programme

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Who are we?

National Hindu Students' Forum (UK), referred to as NHSF (UK), is the data controller for the purposes of the Data Protection Act 1998 and (from 25th May 2018) the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (Data Protection Law). This means that we are responsible for, and control the processing of, your personal information.

This privacy policy specifically covers NHSF (UK) Schools — our secondary-school initiative for students aged 13 to 18 — and this information guide website. It follows the same approach as other NHSF (UK) programme policies, including the Dharmic Games privacy policy.

Safeguarding

The safety of students, families and schools is a priority for NHSF (UK) Schools. Every member of the NHSF (UK) Schools team for 2026/27 holds a valid Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check.

What personal data we collect about you?

We may collect personal information including (non-exhaustive list):

  • Enquiry and contact information: your name, email address, school name, year group, and any message you send through this guide or by email to NHSF (UK) Schools.
  • School and society details: school name, location, zone, teacher or student contact information, and whether a Hindu society is affiliated or still being set up.
  • Event participation: responses to workshop, Hindu Horizons or other Schools event registrations, attendance records, and related notes.
  • Communication data: messages and replies by email, feedback, and support requests.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage information that may be collected automatically by our hosting provider.
  • Media content: images and videos from Schools events, workshops or visits, used for promotional and community purposes where appropriate.

The contact form on this guide does not store your message on the website. It opens a draft email to info@nhsf.org.uk in your own email app. Nothing is sent until you press send.

This includes collecting information about yourself if you get in touch about NHSF (UK) Schools, a school society, or an event by linking your email address to NHSF (UK) via this guide or other NHSF (UK) channels.

Why we use your personal data?

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Schools programme: to support school societies, answer enquiries, and help students, families and schools connect with NHSF (UK).
  • Communication: to reply to messages and keep you informed about relevant Schools events, workshops and updates where you have asked us to.
  • Administrative tasks: to organise visits, sessions and Hindu Horizons, and to keep in touch with school and student contacts.
  • Public relations: to promote NHSF (UK) Schools and share community stories, with care for the age of participants.
  • Record keeping: to keep a record of affiliated societies, enquiries and participation where this is needed to run the programme.
  • Analytics: to understand how this guide is used and to improve it.
  • Safety and safeguarding: to help keep young people safe while taking part in NHSF (UK) Schools activities. Every member of the NHSF (UK) Schools team for 2026/27 holds a valid DBS check.

We do not sell or share your personal details to third parties for marketing purposes. However, if we run an event in partnership with another organisation, your details may need to be shared, and you will be notified in such circumstances.

Sharing your personal data

The personal information we collect about you will be used by us for the purposes listed above. We will never sell or share your personal information with organisations so that they can contact you for any marketing activities.

NHSF (UK) may, however, share your information with our trusted partners and suppliers who work with us to deliver NHSF (UK) Schools, but processing of this information is always carried out under our instruction. Examples include:

  • Schools and teachers, where this is needed to run a society or visit
  • Event venues and facilitators for workshops or Hindu Horizons
  • Media partners for coverage, with consent where required
  • Technical service providers who host or maintain this website

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, or to protect the safety of students and volunteers.

How long do we keep your personal data?

We only keep your personal data as long as is reasonable and necessary for the relevant Schools activities. This typically includes:

  • Active contact: while we are dealing with your enquiry or supporting a school society
  • Programme records: affiliation and society details may be kept so we can run the network
  • Legal requirements: as required by applicable laws and regulations
  • Communication records: emails may be retained for administrative and safeguarding purposes

Your rights and your personal data

Unless subject to an exemption, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Access: the right to request a copy of your personal data which NHSF (UK) holds about you
  • Rectification: the right to request us to correct or change any personal data if it is found to be inaccurate or out of date
  • Right to object: you can object to our processing of your personal information where we are relying on a legitimate interest
  • Restriction: the right to request a restriction is placed on further processing where there is a dispute in relation to the accuracy or processing of your personal data
  • Erasure: the right to request your personal data is erased where it is no longer necessary for NHSF (UK) to retain such data

Please make all requests for access via our email address provided below, and provide us with evidence of your identity. We will provide access without delay, and at latest, within one month of receipt of your request.

Contact details

To exercise all relevant rights, queries or complaints regarding your personal data in relation to NHSF (UK) Schools, please contact us:

Email: info@nhsf.org.uk

Please include “Schools Privacy” in your subject line for faster processing.

Changes to our policy

We shall review this policy from time to time and may update it. If we do so, the updated version will be uploaded on to the website, and will be effective from the moment it is uploaded, so please check it from time to time.
